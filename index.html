Privacy

Thetascape keeps things simple: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

Your sessions, minutes and “how do you feel” answers are saved only in this browser on this device, so you can see your practice. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Clearing your browser data removes them.

The sounds and fonts load from the servers that host this app and from Google Fonts, which can see a standard web request, as any website does.
